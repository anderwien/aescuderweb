Practice has form. It has pacing, contrast, repetition, return, and development. In that sense, practice is already close to composition.

When a musician designs a practice session, they are arranging attention over time. The order of tasks matters. The length of each attempt matters. The silence between attempts matters too.

<aside class="note">
  A good practice structure does not remove difficulty. It gives difficulty a shape.
</aside>

The more intentionally we shape practice, the more likely it is that effort becomes musical understanding rather than only fatigue.
