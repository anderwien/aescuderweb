## Profile

Alejandro Escuder is a pianist, composer, educator, music technologist, orchestrator, and researcher based in Valencia, Spain. His professional work connects stage performance, studio composition, academic teaching, online education, orchestral preparation, sound technology, and interdisciplinary research.

He works across piano, composition, orchestration, music theory, music technology, sound synthesis, psychoacoustics, algorithmic composition, and applied music education.

## Work Experience

#### Music Technology Lecturer

ESMAR, Academia de Alto Rendimiento Musical. September 2022-present.

- Lectures advanced 3rd- and 4th-year students in Music Technology.
- Teaches audio recording, video recording, production, sound synthesis, microphones, coding, acoustics, and technical foundations of sound.
- Designs and curates course materials, workshops, flipped classroom activities, and e-learning workflows.

#### Orchestration, Music Preparation, and Music Supervision

Synchron Stage Vienna, freelance. March 2017-present.

- Music preparation and supervision for recording sessions and concerts.
- Orchestration for series, films, concerts, and orchestral productions.
- Score review, correction, part extraction, arrangements for orchestra, and remote production workflows.
- Selected contexts include Star Wars: The Acolyte, The World of Hans Zimmer, Blue Planet II, Hollywood in Vienna, Life Ball, and Glow and Darkness.

### Music Theory, Harmony, and Piano Teacher

Conservatory contexts and private teaching. September 2019-present.

### Pianist and Improviser
