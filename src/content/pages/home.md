This is a space to explore the intersection of my work. As a **polyfacet artist**, my purpose is to build bridges between **music**, **education**, and **innovation**, crafting experiences that are both intellectually stimulating and emotionally resonant.

I am a Spanish pianist, composer, educator, music technologist, and sociologist. My work moves between the stage, the classroom, the studio, and research, always looking for forms where **beauty**, **clarity**, and **human understanding** can meet.

This website is a living canvas for my work, my thoughts, and my journey.
