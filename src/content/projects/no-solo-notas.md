NoSoloNotas explores music education as a living practice rather than a static collection of rules. It is built around the idea that students need language, examples, structure, and permission to think musically.

The project can include essays, teaching resources, workshops, and guided materials. Its purpose is to make musical understanding more precise and more human at the same time.
