Reflective Practice Studio is a developing framework for musicians who want to understand what happens inside practice sessions. It joins practical routines with reflective prompts, listening notes, and musical decision-making.

The aim is to help musicians leave practice with more than repetition: clearer ears, better questions, and a stronger relationship to the work.
